**An AI dash cam is a vehicle camera with a built-in processor that analyses video in real time to recognise risky situations, warn the driver and automatically save and upload the relevant clip.** Unlike a traditional dash cam, it does not just record. It understands what it sees.

## How AI dash cams work

1. **Capture.** A road-facing camera films the road ahead. Many models add a driver-facing camera with infrared for night-time.
2. **Detect.** Neural networks running on the camera analyse each frame for defined events.
3. **Alert.** When something risky is detected, the driver hears an immediate voice prompt.
4. **Upload.** A short clip, with GPS location and speed, is sent to the cloud for review.
5. **Coach.** Managers and drivers review clips, and patterns feed into safety scores.

Because the AI runs on the device, alerts work even with no network coverage.

## What can they detect?

**Outside the vehicle (ADAS):**

- Forward-collision risk
- Tailgating
- Lane departure
- Rolling through stop signs
- Pedestrians and cyclists in the path

**Inside the vehicle (DMS):**

- Drowsiness and yawning
- Looking away from the road
- Mobile phone use
- Smoking
- Seat belt not worn
- Camera obstruction

## Why fleets adopt them

- **Prevention.** A real-time alert gives the driver a chance to correct before an incident.
- **Evidence.** Video shows what happened and protects drivers who were not at fault.
- **Coaching.** A clip is more persuasive than a number on a report.
- **Efficiency.** Managers review a handful of flagged clips, not hours of footage.

## Addressing driver concerns

Cameras work best with driver support. Explain the purpose openly, emphasise exoneration, use privacy modes for off-duty time, restrict who can view footage and recognise good driving as visibly as you correct bad driving.

## Buyer's checklist

- On-device AI, so alerts work offline
- Accurate detection with a low false-alert rate
- Infrared driver camera for night use
- Automatic upload of event clips
- Video retrieval on demand for a recent period
- Integration with GPS and vehicle data on one platform
- Configurable privacy controls and access permissions
- Over-the-air updates for AI models
- Support for additional cameras where needed
