**A3Track, Samsara and Geotab all provide GPS fleet tracking, AI video safety, maintenance and reporting. The clearest difference is not what the software knows but how your team reaches it.** A3Track puts an AI agent on WhatsApp and on a phone line, in regional languages, and has it call you when an alert needs attention. Samsara and Geotab are larger and longer established, with wide global coverage.

> This comparison is based on information each company has published, checked in November 2026. Products change often. Confirm anything important directly with the vendor before you buy. Samsara and Geotab are trademarks of their respective owners.

## Where the three are similar

If you only need the fundamentals, any of these platforms will serve you:

- **Live tracking** with trip history, geofences and alerts.
- **AI video safety** that detects distraction, drowsiness and tailgating.
- **Vehicle health** from engine data, with service reminders.
- **An AI assistant** that answers questions about your fleet in plain language. Samsara calls its assistant Samsara Assistant. Geotab calls its assistant Geotab Ace.
- **Access from AI tools.** Samsara and Geotab have both announced connectors that let authorised users query fleet data from tools such as ChatGPT. A3Track offers a ChatGPT integration too.

AI inside the dashboard and ChatGPT access are quickly becoming standard. They are reasons to shortlist a vendor, not reasons to choose between these three.

## Where A3Track is different

### 1. The AI agent lives on WhatsApp

Many fleet owners and transport managers in India run their business on WhatsApp. With A3Track you message the agent, "Where is my vehicle AP09CC2318?", and the answer arrives in the same chat with a live map. There is no app to install and no dashboard to learn.

### 2. Voice messages, answered by voice

Not everyone wants to type. Send a voice note and the agent replies with a voice note, plus the map or numbers on screen.

### 3. Your language

Ask in English or in an Indian regional language such as Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi or Bengali. The agent answers in the language you used.

### 4. An AI agent you can phone

Call the A3Track AI call centre at any hour and ask for the status of a vehicle. This matters for owners and customers who will never log in to a dashboard.

### 5. Alerts that call you, and escalate

An app notification is easy to miss at 2 a.m. When a threshold you set is crossed, the A3Track agent places a phone call. If the first person does not answer, it calls the next person in your escalation chain, and records who acknowledged.

### 6. Built India-first

Support for AIS-140 tracking devices, Indian vehicle and trip practices, and pricing designed for Indian fleets come first, not as a later localisation.

## Where Samsara and Geotab are stronger

A fair comparison has to say this plainly.

- **Scale and track record.** Both serve very large numbers of vehicles and have many years of operating history. A3Track is newer.
- **Global coverage.** Both operate across many countries with established support and installation networks. A3Track starts in India.
- **Ecosystem.** Geotab has a large partner marketplace of add-on solutions. Samsara offers a broad range of its own hardware and applications.
- **Regional regulations elsewhere.** For rules such as electronic logging in North America or tachographs in Europe, the global vendors have long-standing solutions.

## Which should you choose?

**A3Track is likely the better fit if:**

- Your fleet operates mainly in India.
- Owners, managers or customers prefer WhatsApp and phone calls to dashboards.
- Your team works in regional languages.
- You want alerts that are impossible to miss.

**Samsara or Geotab may be the better fit if:**

- You operate mainly in North America or Europe.
- You need a specific regional compliance product that A3Track does not yet offer.
- You depend on a particular third-party integration from their marketplaces.

## How to compare properly

1. Write down the three outcomes you care about most.
2. Ask each vendor to demonstrate those outcomes, not a feature tour.
3. Run a pilot on a small group of your own vehicles.
4. Ask the people who will use it every day which one they would keep.
