**A typical GPS tracking system gives you a map, some reports and app notifications. Veyro gives you those too, and then removes the need to open them: you ask an AI agent, in your own language, on the channel you already use.**

Here is what that means in practice.

## A typical GPS tracking system compared with Veyro

| What you want | Typical GPS tracking system | Veyro |
|---|---|---|
| Find a vehicle | Open the app, search, read the map | Message or say "Where is my vehicle?" and get the map back |
| Ask a question | Build or find a report | Ask in plain words on WhatsApp, a call, ChatGPT or the app |
| Use your own language | Usually English menus | English and Indian regional languages, typed or spoken |
| Hear about a problem | App notification, SMS or email | A phone call from the AI agent, escalated until answered |
| Get help at night | Wait for office hours | AI agents answer 24/7 |
| Give an owner or customer access | Create a login and train them | They message or call. Nothing to install |

## 1. Your fleet answers on WhatsApp

Send "Where is my vehicle AP09CC2318?" and the reply comes back in the chat with the vehicle's speed, location and a live map. Follow up with "Fuel level?" or "When will it reach the depot?" in the same conversation.

## 2. You can speak, and it speaks back

Record a voice message. The agent understands it, replies with a voice message and adds the map or figures on screen, so you can listen while driving or working and look later.

## 3. It works in your language

Type or speak in English or in regional languages such as Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi and Bengali. The agent replies in the same language. That opens the system to owners, supervisors and drivers who would never use an English dashboard.

## 4. You can phone it

Call the Veyro AI call centre and ask for any vehicle's status. The agent recognises your registered number, checks what you are allowed to see and answers by voice, at any hour.

## 5. It calls you when something is wrong

Set thresholds for what matters: overspeeding, fuel drain, route deviation, temperature, long stoppage. When one is crossed, the agent phones the responsible person and explains what happened. No answer? It calls the next person in the chain you defined, and logs who acknowledged and when.

## 6. It works inside ChatGPT

If you already use ChatGPT, install the Veyro integration and ask there: "Get me the most fuel consumed vehicle this month." ChatGPT replies with your top vehicles, fuel used, distance and mileage from your live data.

## What has not changed

The foundations still matter, and Veyro covers them: accurate real-time tracking, AI dash cams, fuel monitoring, maintenance, compliance, a full dashboard, Android and iOS apps and an open API. The difference is that you no longer have to go looking for the answer.
