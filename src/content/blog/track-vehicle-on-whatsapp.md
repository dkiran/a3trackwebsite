**WhatsApp vehicle tracking lets you ask where your vehicle is in a WhatsApp chat and get the answer, with a live map, in the same chat.** You don't need to install an app or remember a password. The vehicle carries a normal GPS tracker; WhatsApp is simply the way you talk to the tracking system.

## How it works

1. **A GPS tracker in the vehicle** sends its position to the tracking platform every few seconds.
2. **You send a WhatsApp message** to your provider's number, for example: "Where is my vehicle AP09CC2318?"
3. **An AI agent reads your question,** checks that your phone number is allowed to see that vehicle, and looks up its live data.
4. **You get the answer in the chat:** where the vehicle is, how fast it is moving, and a map. You can ask follow-up questions, such as "How much fuel is left?" or "When will it reach the depot?"

## What you can ask

| You ask | You get |
|---|---|
| "Where is my vehicle AP09CC2318?" | Location, speed and a live map |
| "When will it reach the depot?" | Estimated arrival time and distance |
| "How much fuel is left?" | Fuel level and recent refills or drops |
| "Which vehicles are idle right now?" | A short list with how long each has been idle |
| "Send the customer a tracking link" | A link the customer can open, after you confirm |

## Voice messages and your own language

Typing a vehicle number on a phone while working is not always easy. With Veyro you can send a **voice message** instead, in English or a regional language such as Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi or Bengali. The AI agent understands it and replies with a voice message, plus the map or figures on screen so you can check them later.

## Who it helps most

- **Fleet owners** who want a quick answer without opening a dashboard.
- **Supervisors and dispatchers** working on the move.
- **Transporters** whose customers keep calling to ask where their goods are.
- **Teams who prefer their own language** to an English-only app.

## Keeping it secure

A WhatsApp tracking service should answer only registered phone numbers, show each person only the vehicles they are allowed to see, and ask for confirmation before doing anything that changes something, such as sending a link to a customer. Ask your provider how it does each of these.
