**Telematics helps fleets electrify by showing which vehicles are suitable for replacement, then managing range, charging and battery health once electric vehicles are in service.** The data you already collect from diesel vehicles is the best planning tool you have.

## Before you buy: suitability analysis

Look at real trip data for each existing vehicle:

- Daily distance, including the longest days
- Where and for how long the vehicle parks
- Load carried and terrain
- Time available for charging

Vehicles with predictable routes that return to a depot are natural first candidates: last-mile delivery, staff transport and urban service vehicles.

## Planning charging

Dwell-time data shows where chargers will be used most. Depot charging overnight covers many duty cycles. Routes with long days may need opportunity charging at known stops.

## Operating a mixed fleet

During the transition, most fleets run electric and conventional vehicles together. One platform for both avoids parallel systems. Key EV data points include:

| Data | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| State of charge | Can the vehicle complete its next job? |
| Remaining range | Dispatch and route planning |
| Charging status | Is it plugged in and actually charging? |
| Energy used per km | Efficiency by driver, route and load |
| Battery health | Long-term asset value |

## Managing range in practice

- **Dispatch by charge.** Assign jobs based on real remaining range.
- **Alert on low charge.** Warn drivers and dispatchers before it becomes a problem.
- **Coach efficient driving.** Smooth acceleration and good use of regenerative braking extend range.
- **Account for conditions.** Load, hills, heat and air-conditioning all affect range.

## Smart charging

Charging every vehicle at full power the moment it returns can overload the site and increase electricity cost. Scheduling charging by departure time and tariff period reduces both.

## Protecting the battery

The battery is the most valuable component of an EV. Monitoring charging habits and battery health over time supports warranty claims and resale value.

## Measuring the benefit

Compare energy cost per kilometre, maintenance cost and downtime between electric and conventional vehicles using the same reports. Emissions avoided can be calculated from distance and fuel data for sustainability reporting.
