**Cold chain monitoring is the continuous measurement and recording of temperature for perishable goods, from the point of origin to final delivery, with alerts when conditions go out of range.** Its purpose is simple: protect the product and prove that you did.

## Why it matters

Food, dairy, pharmaceuticals, vaccines and many chemicals lose quality or become unsafe when exposed to the wrong temperature. A single failure can mean a rejected load, a lost customer and a safety risk.

## Typical temperature bands

| Category | Typical range | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Frozen | −18 °C or below | Frozen food, ice cream |
| Chilled | 0 °C to 5 °C | Dairy, fresh meat, many vaccines |
| Cool | 8 °C to 15 °C | Some fruit, vegetables, confectionery |
| Controlled ambient | 15 °C to 25 °C | Many medicines |

Always follow the range specified for the particular product.

## Where the cold chain breaks

- **Loading and unloading.** Doors open, product waits on the dock.
- **Door openings on multi-drop routes.** Each stop lets warm air in.
- **Refrigeration faults.** Unit failure, low fuel or incorrect set-point.
- **Poor loading.** Blocked airflow creates warm spots.
- **Unit switched off.** To save fuel or reduce noise.

## How real-time monitoring works

1. **Sensors** in each compartment measure temperature, and often humidity.
2. **Door sensors** record every opening.
3. **A gateway** in the vehicle collects readings wirelessly and adds GPS location.
4. **The cloud platform** checks readings against limits for each load.
5. **Alerts** go to the driver and the control room the moment a limit is crossed.
6. **Reports** provide a complete record for each trip and delivery.

## Setting it up well

- Place sensors where air returns to the unit and at the warmest point of the load space.
- Set alert thresholds with a short delay, so brief door openings do not cause false alarms.
- Define who responds to an alert and what they should do.
- Calibrate sensors on a regular schedule.
- Share delivery temperature reports with customers automatically.

## Beyond the vehicle

The same sensors can monitor cold rooms and warehouses, giving one continuous record from storage to doorstep.
